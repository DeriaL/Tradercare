# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prop traders who pass paid challenges to trade firm capital, and systematic traders running several MetaTrader / cTrader accounts. They land here from prop-firm communities and search, often on a phone, sceptical of "trading tools" that sell signals.

## Product Purpose

Traders Care helps a trader trade by their own rules and see the real cost of every deviation: an auto-synced journal, analytics, live prop-rule monitoring, reviews and an AI assistant that reads the trader's own data. The landing introduces the product and converts to a free account.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, candid, exact. Data over hype, no signals, no promises of profit. Speaks to the trader as a peer, in plain Ukrainian with correct trading terms.

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing pages (purple gradients, three identical feature cards, fake testimonials), crypto-casino neon, signal-seller pages with profit screenshots.

## Design Principles

1. Show the product working with one consistent demo dataset instead of describing it.
2. Every number is either real product fact or clearly sample data.
3. Money colours mean money: green and red only for P&L.
4. One accent, used as a signal, never as decoration.
5. Motion explains a state change (sync, deviation, rule breach), never loops for show.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard paths, prefers-reduced-motion respected everywhere, Cyrillic-first typography, layouts that survive 20-40% longer RU/UA strings.
